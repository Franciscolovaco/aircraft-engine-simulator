# Physics Reference Guide

## Introduction
This document serves as a reference for the physics principles used in the aircraft engine simulator.

## Newton's Laws of Motion
1. **First Law**: An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion unless acted upon by a net external force.
2. **Second Law**: The acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass (F = ma).
3. **Third Law**: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

## Energy Principles
- **Kinetic Energy**: The energy of an object due to its motion, calculated as KE = 1/2 mv².
- **Potential Energy**: The stored energy of position, calculated as PE = mgh.

## Thermodynamics
- **First Law**: Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed.
- **Second Law**: The total entropy of an isolated system can never decrease over time.

## Fluid Dynamics
- **Bernoulli's Equation**: Describes the conservation of energy in a flowing fluid.
- **Continuity Equation**: A1V1 = A2V2, where A is the cross-sectional area and V is the fluid velocity.

## Conclusion
This guide is intended to provide quick references and formulas for understanding the physical principles applied within the simulation.
